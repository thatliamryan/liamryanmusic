WHAT TO EXPECT

What tutoring looks like with me!

01

Getting in touch

Tell me a little about yourself, what you're working on, and what you'd like help with using the Contact Me page above or by reaching out to me directly at liamryan@gmail.com. I'll get back to you via email and send a Calendly link to schedule a free 15-minute introductory call via Zoom.

02

Your first lesson

All tutoring sessions will be conducted via Zoom. You can schedule all lessons using Calendly and we'll start working through material that's relevant to you! Bring an assignment, exam, piece of music, or simply a question you'd like to go over.

03

Keep building your skills

From there, lessons will continue to adapt to your goals. We'll build on what you've learned and adjust as your needs develop.

Choosing a session or package

Before our first lesson, we'll start with a complimentary 15-minute Zoom to get to know each other and make a game plan.

A single session is a good choice if you have a specific assignment, exam, or question you'd like to work through, or if you'd simply like to try a lesson before committing to a regular schedule.

Consistency is key! When you'd like to meet on a regular schedule, the 5- and 10-session packages offer a discounted rate. Packages are for students who know they want to work toward a larger goal or build their skills over the semester.

Payment is due at the first scheduled lesson. For session packages, payment for the full package is due by the first lesson. I accept Venmo and Zelle, and payment information will be provided after our introductory Zoom.

All students receive a complimentary 15-minute introductory call before beginning lessons. Please note that the calendar fills quickly around mid-terms (October) and before finals (late November/early December) so I encourage you to schedule those lessons as soon as possible.
