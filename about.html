ABOUT LIAM

Music is fun!

I'm a vocalist, musician, and music educator with a particular interest in helping people understand what's happening underneath the music they hear. I've always found theory most interesting when it stops being a collection of rules and starts becoming a way of describing something you can actually hear. A chord progression isn't just a series of Roman numerals. An interval isn't just a distance between two notes. Once you can connect the written idea to the sound, music theory becomes much more intuitive. That's the approach I bring into my lessons. My musical education began with performing. I spent years singing in choirs and ensembles before becoming increasingly interested in jazz, harmony, improvisation, and composition. I'm currently completing my graduate studies in jazz voice, where I've had the opportunity to study performance, arranging, composition, ear training, and advanced harmony. Working across these areas has shaped the way I think about theory. I don't see notation, harmony, ear training, and performance as separate subjects. They're different ways of understanding the same music. I've always enjoyed the moment when something that initially seems complicated suddenly clicks.
