ABOUT LIAM

Hello!

My name is Liam Ryan and I look forward to working with you.

Born and raised in Chicago, I received my Bachelor of Music in Jazz Vocal Performance from the University of Miami's Frost School of Music, and my Master of Music in Jazz Vocal Performance from Manhattan School of Music. I am proud to be a National YoungArts Foundation and Downbeat Magazine Student Award winner for jazz voice.

When I am not teaching, you may catch me singing jazz tunes at Candlelight Sinatra. I love music from the Great American Songbook, and my favorite composers are Jerome Kern, Frank Loesser, Harold Arlen, Cole Porter, and Rodgers & Hammerstein.

My Teaching Philosophy Before I sang jazz, I was a theatre kid! I grew up performing in musicals with children’s theatre, through high school, and in college. So I understand how a solid foundation in music theory is essential in giving you the skills to: *Sight-read and learn music quickly *Work with musical directors and accompanists *Harmonize in ensembles *Explore emotions in song and artistry on stage

My studies in pedagogy have shaped how I think about making music and helping others understand it. Teaching has become just as central to my work as performing. For the past four years, I've tutored students in both music theory and computer science — and working across those two subjects has taught me a lot about how people learn. It's given me a range of ways to explain ideas, so I can find the explanation that actually clicks for you.

I work with students of all ages and experience levels, and I will always meet you where you are. Whether you're starting out with the basics, working through advanced coursework, or you simply love music and want to understand it more deeply, my lessons are built around your individual needs. This includes prep for AP Music Theory, placement tests, auditions, and college exams, as well as longer-term support as you work toward a specific goal.

Music theory is cumulative — each concept tends to build on the last, so a class that keeps moving at a fixed pace doesn't always leave room to fully close a gap before the next topic arrives. We will find exactly where you need extra support and then work together until it genuinely clicks. Feel free to bring homework, study guides, or past exams, and we'll build lessons around what you actually need. Everything moves at your pace.

My goal isn't just to help you get through the next assignment — it's to help you build a real, comfortable understanding of music theory so you can be the best artist you can be!
