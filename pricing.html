Choosing a session or package

A single session is a good choice if you have a specific assignment, exam, or question you'd like to work through, or if you'd simply like to try a lesson before committing to a regular schedule.

If you already know you'd like to continue with tutoring, the 5- and 10-session packages offer a discounted rate. Packages are for students who know they want to work consistently toward a larger goal or build their skills over time.

All students receive a free 15-minute introductory call before beginning lessons.
